APPLICATION DESCRIPTION :
# DailyDabba — Smart Tiffin Management Service

**DailyDabba** is a smart and user-friendly **Tiffin Management Service** designed to simplify the way customers discover, subscribe to, and manage daily tiffin services while helping tiffin vendors efficiently manage their meals, customers, subscriptions, and deliveries.

The platform brings **tiffin service providers and customers together in a single digital ecosystem**. Customers can explore available tiffin services, view meal plans, compare subscription options, manage their subscriptions, and keep track of their daily meals. Vendors, on the other hand, can use the platform to manage their tiffin offerings, customer subscriptions, orders, and day-to-day operations from one centralized system.

A major goal of DailyDabba is to replace the traditional, largely manual process of managing tiffin services. Many local tiffin businesses still rely on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, notebooks, or spreadsheets to handle subscriptions and customer information. This can make it difficult to keep track of active subscriptions, meal preferences, cancellations, deliveries, and payments. DailyDabba aims to provide a structured digital solution that reduces this complexity.

The application follows a **role-based architecture**, where both customers and vendors are represented as users while their capabilities depend on their role within the platform. Authentication and authorization are handled securely using **JWT-based authentication**, allowing protected resources to be accessed only by authenticated and authorized users.

For customers, DailyDabba can provide features such as **user registration and login, profile management, tiffin service discovery, meal-plan browsing, subscription management, meal scheduling, and order tracking**. The system can help customers manage their daily tiffin requirements without repeatedly communicating with the vendor.

For vendors, the platform provides functionality for **creating and managing tiffin plans, managing customers and subscriptions, handling daily orders, and monitoring their service operations**. This allows vendors to organize their business digitally and maintain better visibility over their customers and daily workload.

The frontend of DailyDabba is designed with a **clean, minimalistic Neomorphic UI**, combining soft shadows, rounded components, and subtle depth while maintaining usability and accessibility. The primary visual palette uses **white, dark grey, and a yellow-green accent**, creating a modern and recognizable identity. The frontend is being developed using **React and Tailwind CSS**.

The backend is built using **Spring Boot**, following a layered architecture that separates controllers, services, repositories, business logic, DTOs, security, and persistence concerns. The application uses **Spring Security and JWT** for authentication and authorization and integrates with a relational database for persistent storage.

Overall, DailyDabba is intended to be more than just an online tiffin ordering system. It is a **digital management platform for the complete tiffin-service lifecycle**, connecting customers and vendors while making everyday tiffin management simpler, more organized, and more efficient.

DESIGN STYLE :
Design the UI for **DailyDabba**, a modern tiffin-management platform, using a **clean, minimalistic, refined neumorphic design system**.

This design specification is GLOBAL and MUST remain consistent across every page, component, modal, form, dashboard, card, navbar, sidebar, authentication screen, and responsive layout generated for the application.

## 1. Overall Visual Direction

Use a **modern, subtle neumorphism style** — soft, tactile, spacious, and professional.

The interface should feel:

* Clean
* Minimal
* Calm
* Premium
* Friendly
* Food-service oriented
* Modern but not futuristic
* Soft and tactile
* Easy to scan and use

Avoid excessive decoration, gradients, glassmorphism, excessive borders, strong shadows, visual clutter, and unnecessary animations.

The neumorphism should be **subtle rather than exaggerated**.

The UI should look like a real production SaaS/product interface, not a Dribbble concept.

---

## 2. Color System

Use ONLY this primary color family:

### White

Primary surface/background color.

Use for:

* Main page backgrounds
* Cards
* Form containers
* Panels
* Modals
* Navigation surfaces where appropriate

The white should be slightly softened rather than extremely harsh pure white when necessary, but maintain a predominantly white appearance.

### Dark Grey

Use dark grey for:

* Primary text
* Headings
* Icons
* Navigation text
* Important labels
* Strong UI emphasis

Prefer dark charcoal/dark grey instead of pure black.

Avoid using pure black unless absolutely necessary.

### Yellow + Green

Use a **yellow-green / fresh food-inspired accent family** for:

* Primary CTA buttons
* Active navigation states
* Important highlights
* Success states
* Selected states
* Food-related visual accents
* Small decorative elements

The yellow and green should feel naturally blended and fresh, inspired by:

* Fresh vegetables
* Healthy food
* Warm homemade meals

Do NOT introduce unrelated colors such as blue, purple, pink, red, or orange into the primary visual system.

For warnings/errors, use the minimum necessary semantic variation while keeping the overall UI visually consistent.

---

## 3. Neumorphic Surface System

Use white/light surfaces with **soft dual-direction shadows** to create depth.

Typical neumorphic appearance:

* Soft dark shadow on the lower/right side
* Soft light highlight on the upper/left side
* Large blur radius
* Low opacity
* No harsh black shadows

Example conceptual Tailwind shadow:

`shadow-[8px_8px_18px_rgba(55,65,81,0.12),-8px_-8px_18px_rgba(255,255,255,0.9)]`

Do not apply extremely strong shadows.

Neumorphism should communicate hierarchy through **light, shadow, spacing, and elevation**, not borders.

Prefer:

* Soft raised cards
* Soft inset inputs
* Soft raised buttons
* Subtle pressed states

Avoid:

* Thick borders
* Heavy outlines
* Hard drop shadows
* Excessive floating elements

---

## 4. Page Background

Use a soft white/off-white background throughout the application.

The page should provide enough contrast for neumorphic surfaces while remaining visually bright and clean.

Use generous whitespace.

Do not fill large areas with dark colors.

Dark grey should primarily be used for typography and selected interface elements.

---

## 5. Typography

Use a clean modern sans-serif font.

Preferred typography characteristics:

* Highly readable
* Medium letter spacing
* Clear hierarchy
* Rounded/modern appearance where possible

Use approximately:

* Large page heading: `text-3xl` to `text-4xl`
* Section heading: `text-xl` to `text-2xl`
* Card heading: `text-lg`
* Body text: `text-sm` to `text-base`
* Supporting text: `text-xs` to `text-sm`

Headings should use dark grey and strong font weight.

Avoid overly decorative typography.

Do not use serif fonts.

---

## 6. Spacing

Use a generous spacing system.

Prefer:

* `p-6`
* `p-8`
* `gap-4`
* `gap-6`
* `gap-8`

Maintain consistent horizontal and vertical rhythm between sections.

Avoid sharp rectangular components unless required by the content.

Do not use excessively circular UI elements unless they are icons, avatars, or compact controls.
